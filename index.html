What I’d build first at Fredrin

  1. 01 / Find the moment a developer gets real value.

    I would start by defining a successful first experience: a developer creates a ticket, gets a Worker running, reviews the output, and comes back. I would instrument those steps, verify which events Fredrin already exposes, and tie CRM and lifecycle follow-up to that behavior.

  2. 02 / Separate curious individuals from teams that are adopting.

    I would pair that with account-level signals like teammate invites and repeat usage, so follow-up for a solo developer looks different from follow-up for a team that is spreading the product.

  3. 03 / Build acquisition around developer intent.

    I would test engineering hiring and public GitHub activity as account signals, and publish comparison pages against Cursor, Devin, and Vibe Kanban. I would track which pages lead to activated users, not just visits.
